01 — Project Overview

The Vision

The Thrive WELLthy project was a strategic initiative to establish a digital storefront on Shopify. My objective was to create a seamless, elegant shopping experience that effectively manages the sale of perishables alongside a dedicated Sea Moss skincare line.

The final platform reflects natural integrity while simplifying the customer journey from initial browsing to local fulfillment, ensuring a premium feel at every touchpoint.

Deliverables
Brand Identity, Shopify Theme Build, Inventory Setup, Fulfillment Logistics
Core Tech
Shopify Liquid, Tailwind CSS, Brand Strategy
Role
Brand Strategist & E-commerce Developer
